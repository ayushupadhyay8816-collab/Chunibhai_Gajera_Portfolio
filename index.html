01 / ABOUT

A journey built on persistence and purpose

Chunibhai (Chuni) Gajera is a Gujarati entrepreneur and philanthropist, born on 5 July 1960 in Amreli, Gujarat. His official biography describes how he moved to Surat in 1972 and began working in the diamond industry. Alongside his elder brother, Vasantbhai Gajera, he later helped build Laxmi Diamond into a major diamond-export business.

Beyond business, Gajera is associated with social initiatives through the Smt. Shantaben Haribhai Gajera Charitable Trust. The Trust focuses on education, healthcare and community service. Its initiatives include schools and colleges, healthcare support, blood-donation drives and Vatsalyadham, a home that provides care and education to children.

His public profile highlights the values of knowledge, action and care, along with the idea of “One Happiness”—building a society where people have opportunities to learn, grow and contribute. This is an educational overview, not an official website.

02 / EDUCATION & SOCIAL IMPACT

Building opportunities through education

Chunibhai Gajera is associated with the Smt. Shantaben Haribhai Gajera Charitable Trust, which works across education, healthcare and community service. The Trust’s official website describes a network of 9 campuses, 18 schools and 3 colleges, serving more than 58,000 learners. These are Trust-wide figures and may change over time.

Holistic learning

The Trust describes an approach that supports academic learning alongside creativity, curiosity, responsibility and personal development.

Technical & professional education

Laxmi Vidyapeeth at Sarigam includes schools and professional colleges. The official pages list programmes such as engineering, architecture and management.

STEM and making

Sunita’s Makerspace offers opportunities for learners to explore tools, projects, mentors and practical experimentation.

Education for girls

The Smt. Shantaben Haribhai Gajera Shaikshanik Sankul in Amreli includes schools, colleges and distance-learning centres, with a focus on expanding education opportunities for girls.

Medical education

Smt. Shantabaa Medical College and General Hospital in Amreli combines medical education with healthcare services.

Learning and care at Vatsalyadham

Vatsalyadham provides a home and educational support for children, helping residents work toward independence and community participation.
